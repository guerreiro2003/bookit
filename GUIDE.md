# 📖 BookIt — Complete User Guide

Everything you need to know to use Book It, no matter who you are.

Three sections in this guide:

- 👤 **For Clients** — people making bookings
- 👨‍💼 **For Staff** — collaborators working at the salon
- 🔧 **For Admins** — the business owner

Jump straight to your section.

---

# 👤 For Clients

If you want to **book a service at the salon**, this is your section.

## How to make a booking (no account needed)

You don't need an account to book. Anyone can book in less than a minute.

### Step 1 — Open the booking page

🌐 Open the link the salon gave you. It looks something like:
`https://thesalon.netlify.app/?salon=zenorganic`

You'll see:
- The salon's name and logo at the top
- A progress bar with 5 steps (Service → Team → Date → Data → Confirm)
- A list of services

> 💡 **Tip:** If you've been here before and have an account, you'll see a green bar at the top saying "Sessão ativa" (active session) and your name.

> ⏰ **Heads up:** A small popup might appear after 4 seconds inviting you to join the "Zen Club" (loyalty program). You can close it by clicking the **X** in the corner or by clicking "Continuar sem conta" (continue without an account).

### Step 2 — Pick a service

You'll see all the services the salon offers. Each card shows:
- 💇 The service name
- ⏱ How long it takes
- 💰 The price
- Sometimes a **green "Pacote"** badge (which means it's a combination deal — multiple services for a discount)

👆 **Click the service you want.** The screen automatically moves to the next step.

### Step 3 — Choose a team member (or "no preference")

You'll see a list of available staff members.
- The first option, **"Sem preferência"** (No preference), means the salon will assign whoever is free.
- Or pick a specific person by clicking their card.

👆 **Click the option you want.**

### Step 4 — Pick a date and time

A calendar appears. The rules:
- 🚫 Past dates: greyed out, can't be selected
- 🚫 Days when the salon is closed: greyed out
- 🟢 Today: has a small dot underneath
- ✨ Available days: blue when you hover

👆 **Click the day you want.**

Now you'll see all the available time slots for that day.
- ✅ White slots: available
- ❌ Greyed-out slots with a line through: already booked

👆 **Click the time you want.**

Then click the **"Continuar →"** button at the bottom.

### Step 5 — Your details

Fill in the form:
- **Nome completo** (Full name) — required
- **Email** — required
- **Telemóvel** (Phone) — required, used to confirm/remind
- **Esta marcação é para outra pessoa?** (Booking for someone else?) — optional. If you're booking on behalf of someone (e.g., a parent for a child), write that person's name here.
- **Código de referido** (Referral code) — optional. If a friend gave you a code, type it. You both get a discount!
- **Notas** (Notes) — optional. Tell the salon anything they should know (e.g., "please use cruelty-free products" or "I'm coming with my daughter").

> 💡 If you have an account and are logged in, the name/email/phone are filled in automatically.

Click **"Continuar →"**.

### Step 6 — Confirm

A summary appears with everything you chose. Double-check it.

If you used a referral code that's valid, you'll see a **"Desconto aplicado"** notice with the discount.

👆 **If everything looks right, click "Confirmar marcação".**

⏳ Wait 1-2 seconds. The booking is being created in the system.

### What happens next

🎉 You're redirected to a **confirmation page** showing:
- Your booking details (service, date, time, price)
- Your **referral code** (a unique code only you have, like `ZEN-PEDR42`)
- An invitation to create an account

> ⚠️ Your booking starts as **"Pendente"** (Pending). The salon needs to confirm it. You'll get an email when they do.

## Should I create an account?

**You don't have to**, but it's worth it because:
- 🌟 You get **points (Zen Club)** for every paid visit — after 10 visits, you get a discount on your next one
- 🎂 If you add your **birthday**, you get a special discount in your birth month automatically
- 🔗 You get a **referral code** — every friend who uses it gets a discount, and so do you
- 📋 You can see your **booking history** and **cancel** future bookings
- ⚡ Next bookings are faster (data filled in automatically)

### How to create an account

1. Go to: `https://thesalon.netlify.app/account.html?salon=zenorganic`
2. Click the **"Criar conta"** (Create account) tab.
3. Fill in: name, email, phone, password (minimum 6 characters).
4. Optionally add your birthday — recommended for the birthday discount.
5. Click **"Criar conta"**.

You're in. The system will automatically link your past bookings (made with the same email) to your account.

## How to view or cancel your bookings

1. Go to **`account.html?salon=...`** and log in.
2. You'll see 5 tabs at the top:
   - **Próximas** (Upcoming) — future bookings
   - **Histórico** (History) — past bookings
   - **Descontos** (Discounts) — discount codes you have
   - **Referidos** (Referrals) — friends you've brought
   - **Dados** (Data) — your profile

### Cancel a booking

1. In the **"Próximas"** tab, find the booking you want to cancel.
2. Click the red **"Cancelar"** button.
3. Confirm.

✅ Done. You'll see a toast saying "Marcação cancelada". The salon is notified automatically.

> ⚠️ **Cancel in advance.** If you don't show up without cancelling, the salon can mark you as "Não compareceu" and deduct 5 points from your account.

## Tips and tricks

- 💡 **Lost your referral code?** Open the **"Referidos"** tab in your account.
- 💡 **Use a discount code from the salon?** Go to **"Descontos"** → paste the code → click **"Verificar"**. If valid, mention it to the staff when you arrive.
- 🌙 **Dark mode** is automatic if your phone/computer is set to dark mode.
- 🗑 **Delete your account** under **"Dados"** → "Apagar a minha conta" (your visit history stays anonymized for the salon's records).

# 👨‍💼 For Staff Members

If you **work at the salon**, this is your section. Your job: see today's appointments, confirm them, register payments, and handle no-shows.

## How to log in

🌐 Open: `https://thesalon.netlify.app/staff.html?salon=zenorganic`

You'll see two tabs at the top: **Equipa** (Team) and **Admin**.

- 👉 **You** are "Equipa". Stay on this tab.
- 🔑 Type the **team password** (your owner gives you this).
- Click **"Entrar"**.

You're in. Your session lasts 8 hours, then you'll need to log in again.

> ⚠️ **Don't share the team password.** If you leave the salon, the owner will change it.

## The Staff Portal

After logging in, you see 3 tabs at the top:

### Tab 1 — Hoje (Today)

This is your **main screen**. It shows everything happening today.

At the top: 4 numbers (stats)
- 📅 **Marcações** — total bookings today (excluding cancelled)
- ⏳ **Pendentes** — pending bookings (haven't been confirmed yet)
- ✅ **Confirmadas** — confirmed
- 💰 **Faturação** — money already paid today

Below: a list of each booking with the customer's name, service, time, status, and a few action buttons.

🟢 **Real-time:** the page updates automatically. If a customer makes a booking right now, it appears here within 1-2 seconds without you doing anything.

### Tab 2 — Todas as marcações (All bookings)

Past + future bookings, filtered.

- 🔍 **Search bar** — filter by name, phone, or service.
- 🏷️ **Status dropdown** — filter by status (pending, confirmed, etc.)
- 📅 **Date picker** — show only a specific date.
- ❌ **Clear** — remove filters.

### Tab 3 — Serviços (Services)

Just shows the menu of services with prices and duration. **Read only** — you can't change them from here. If something needs updating, ask the owner.

## What each action button does

Each booking has 1-4 buttons depending on status:

### ✓ Confirmar (Confirm)

**Shows when:** booking is "Pendente".
**Does:** changes status to "Confirmada".
**When to use:** when the customer's appointment is set in stone (you've checked the schedule and want to lock it in).

### 💰 Pagamento (Payment) — **The most important button**

**Shows when:** booking is not yet paid AND not cancelled/noshow.
**Does:** opens a popup to register the payment.

🚨 **This is the critical step.** Without it, the customer doesn't get loyalty points.

How to register a payment:
1. Click the green **"Pagamento"** button on the booking.
2. A popup opens with two methods:
   - 💵 **Balcão** — paid in person (cash, debit card, multibanco)
   - 💳 **Online** — paid through online platform
3. Click the method the customer used.
4. The "Confirmar" button at the bottom becomes active. Click it.
5. ✅ Booking is now "Concluída" (Completed) and the customer earned **10 loyalty points**.

### Não compareceu (No-show)

**Shows when:** booking is pending or confirmed.
**Does:** marks the customer as a no-show. **Deducts 5 points** from their account.
**When to use:** ONLY if the customer didn't come and didn't cancel ahead of time.

> ⚠️ **Be fair.** If a customer warned you in person or by phone, cancel instead of marking as no-show. No-show hurts their loyalty status.

### Cancelar (Cancel)

**Shows when:** any non-completed booking.
**Does:** marks the booking as cancelled. **Doesn't deduct points.**
**When to use:** when the customer cancelled in advance or you need to reschedule.

> 💡 **Cancel = no penalty. No-show = penalty.** Be careful with this distinction.

## What each status means

| Status | What it means | What you should do |
|---|---|---|
| 🟡 **Pendente** | Customer booked, salon hasn't confirmed yet | Confirm or cancel |
| 🔵 **Confirmada** | Salon confirmed, waiting for customer | When customer arrives & pays → Register payment |
| 🟢 **Concluída** | Done — customer came and paid | Nothing more to do |
| ⚪ **Cancelada** | Cancelled — no penalty | Nothing to do |
| 🔴 **Não compareceu** | Customer didn't show up | Already done — points deducted |

## A typical day

1. Open `staff.html` in the morning. Log in.
2. Check the **"Hoje"** tab — see how many appointments you have.
3. Confirm any **pending** bookings (review them with the owner if needed).
4. As each customer arrives → check them off mentally, do the service.
5. When they pay → click **💰 Pagamento** → select method → confirm.
6. If someone doesn't show up after 15 min → mark as **"Não compareceu"**.
7. End of day → check stats: "Faturação" tells you how much the salon made.

## Tips

- 💡 **Phone bookings**: if a customer calls to book, ask the owner (admin) to add the booking manually in the admin panel.
- 🌙 **Dark mode**: click the moon icon at the top to switch.
- 🆘 **Logged out?** Sessions last 8 hours. Just re-enter the team password.
- 📱 **Use it on mobile**: the portal works perfectly on phones.

# 🔧 For Admins (business owner)

You own the salon. **Everything is in your hands.** This section walks through every panel of the admin dashboard.

## How to log in

🌐 Open: `https://thesalon.netlify.app/admin.html`

Type the **email** and **password** you set during the initial setup. Click **"Entrar"**.

If you forgot the password, click **"Esqueci-me da password"** to get a reset email.

## Dashboard — your home screen

When you log in, you land on the **Dashboard**.

### What you see

At the top of the page:
- 📅 **Date** — today's date
- 🔍 **Search bar** with `⌘K` hint — opens a quick-access menu (try clicking it or pressing Ctrl+K)
- **🔄 Atualizar** — refresh button
- **➕ Nova marcação** — create a booking manually

5 cards with **today's statistics**:
- **Marcações hoje** — total appointments today
- **Pendentes** — waiting for confirmation
- **Confirmadas** — confirmed
- **Faturação hoje** — money earned (paid bookings only)
- **Total clientes** — your total customer base

Below: **Marcações de hoje** — table with everyone coming today.

🟢 **Real-time** — these numbers update automatically when new bookings come in.

> 💡 **First time logging in?** You'll see a **welcome card** at the top guiding you through 4 setup steps. Click **"Começar →"** to start.

## Side menu — every section explained

The menu on the left has 4 groups:

### 🏠 Geral
- **Dashboard** — the home screen

### ⚙️ Operação (Operations)
- **Marcações** — every booking (today + past + future)
- **Vista de dia** — calendar view of one day
- **Clientes** — every customer

### 🔧 Configurar (Configure)
- **Serviços** — what you offer
- **Equipa** — your team
- **Horários** — opening hours
- **Promoções** — promotional banners
- **Fidelização** — loyalty program settings
- **Configurações** — salon info, branding, team password

### 🔗 Footer of menu
- **Site marcações** — open the public booking page
- **Portal equipa** — open the staff portal
- **Tema escuro/claro** — toggle dark mode
- **Sair** — log out

## Marcações (Bookings) panel

The full list of every booking, sortable, filterable, exportable.

### What you can do

1. **Filter by status** — show only Pending, Confirmed, Completed, Cancelled, or No-shows.
2. **Filter by date** — show only a specific day.
3. **Search** — by customer name, phone, or service name.
4. **Export CSV** — download all bookings to a spreadsheet.
5. **Create new booking** — click **"+ Nova"**.

### Action buttons on each booking

Same as the staff portal:
- ✓ **Confirmar** — accept a pending booking
- 💰 **Pagamento** — register payment (gives customer points)
- **Não compareceu** — customer didn't show up
- ❌ **Cancelar** — cancel the booking

### Creating a booking manually

Use this when a customer calls or walks in.

1. Click **"➕ Nova marcação"** in the top right (or in the dashboard).
2. A popup opens. Fill in:
   - **Cliente** (Name) — required
   - **Telefone** — required
   - **Email** — optional (recommended if you want to link to their existing account)
   - **Serviço** — required, pick from dropdown
   - **Colaborador** — optional, default is "no preference"
   - **Data** and **Hora** — required
   - **Notas** — optional
3. Click **"Criar marcação"**.

> ⚠️ **Conflict warning:** if you try to book the same staff at the same time as another booking, a warning pops up asking if you want to continue.

## Vista de dia (Day view) panel

A visual schedule of one day. Useful to see who's coming when and gaps in the day.

- **Navigate days** with the **‹** / **›** arrows.
- **Jump to today** with the **"Hoje"** button.
- **Pick a date** in the date input.

Each event is colored by status:
- 🟡 Yellow — pending
- 🔵 Blue — confirmed
- 🟢 Green — completed (paid)
- ⚪ Grey — cancelled

👆 **Click an event** to open the payment modal directly.

## Clientes (Clients) panel

Every customer who's ever booked.

For each one you see:
- Name, email
- **Visits** count
- **Points** (Zen Club)
- **Total spent** (lifetime value)
- **Loyalty progress bar** (visits towards next reward)
- Their **referral code**

🔍 **Search** by name, email, or referral code.

📥 **Export CSV** — download to spreadsheet for marketing campaigns.

## Serviços (Services) panel — what you offer

This is where you manage your menu.

### Add a service

1. In the form at the top, fill in:
   - **Nome** (Name): e.g., "Corte + Brushing"
   - **Preço (€)**: e.g., `35`
   - **Duração (min)**: e.g., `45`
   - **Tipo** (Type):
     - **Individual** — a single service
     - **Pacote / Combinação** — a combo with savings
2. **Descrição** (optional) — extra info shown on the booking page.
3. If **Pacote**, fill in:
   - **Preço original** — the price without the package deal
   - **Inclui** — what's in the package (e.g., "Corte + Coloração + Tratamento")
4. Click **"Adicionar serviço"**.

✅ The service appears immediately on the public booking page.

### Edit or remove a service

In the services list:
- **Editar** — fills the form with the service so you can change it
- **Desativar/Ativar** — hide it from the public page (but keep the data)
- **Remover** — delete it permanently (you can undo within 6 seconds)

> 💡 **Tip:** to update a price, click "Editar", change the number, click "Guardar alterações". The change is live instantly.

## Equipa (Staff) panel

Your team.

### Add a staff member

1. Fill in:
   - **Nome** — required
   - **Função** (Role) — e.g., "Cabeleireira"
   - **Especialidades** — what they're great at, comma-separated
   - **Email** — optional
   - **Sobre** (Bio) — optional, shown to customers
2. Click **"Adicionar colaborador"**.

### Set individual schedules

If a team member doesn't follow the salon's general schedule (e.g., works only on weekends or has a day off):

1. In the **"Horário por colaborador"** section, pick the staff member.
2. A schedule editor appears. Set times for each day or check **"Folga"** to mark a day off.
3. Click **"Guardar horário do colaborador"**.

> 💡 When customers book and pick this team member, they'll only see slots when this person is working.

### Deactivate or remove a staff member

- **Desativar** — hides them from the public booking page but keeps their data.
- **Remover** — deletes them. Their past bookings stay attributed.

> ⚠️ When someone leaves the salon, **change the team password** in Configurações.

## Horários (Hours) panel

Set the general opening hours.

For each day of the week:
- **Time fields** — opening and closing time
- **"Fechado"** (Closed) checkbox — closes the day entirely

Click **"Guardar horário"** when done.

📝 **Effect:** customers will only see available slots within these hours. Closed days won't appear in the calendar.

## Promoções (Promotions) panel

Banners that appear at the top of the public booking page.

### Add a promotion

1. **Título** — short headline (e.g., "Primavera em beleza")
2. **Descrição** — one-line explanation (e.g., "20% em coloração durante abril")
3. **Badge** — optional small label (e.g., "-20%")
4. Click **"Publicar promoção"**.

### Manage promotions

- **Desativar/Ativar** — hide without deleting
- **Remover** — delete

## Fidelização (Loyalty) panel

The customer rewards program.

- **Visitas para desconto** — how many paid visits before a discount is given (default 10).
- **Desconto (%)** — the % discount they get (default 20).
- **Desconto referido (%)** — discount when someone uses a referral code (default 10).
- **Pontos por "não compareceu"** — points deducted from a no-show customer (default 5). Set to 0 to disable.

Click **"Guardar"**.

## Configurações (Settings) panel

The general info about your salon.

- **Nome do salão** — name (shown on every page)
- **Tagline** — short slogan
- **Morada, Telefone, Email** — contact info
- **Cor principal** — your brand color. The whole site uses this color for buttons, accents, etc.
- **Desconto de aniversário (%)** — automatic discount in the customer's birth month
- **Password da equipa** — the password your team uses on `staff.html`. **Change it whenever a member leaves.**

Click **"Guardar configurações"**.

## Power user shortcuts

- ⌨️ **`⌘K` (Mac) / `Ctrl+K` (Windows)** — opens the **command palette**: a quick search for navigation and clients. Type a section name or customer's name and press Enter.
- ⌨️ **Esc** — closes any modal or popup.
- 🌙 **Dark mode** — bottom of the sidebar, "Tema escuro/claro".
- 🔄 **Real-time** — every page refreshes itself when something changes.
- ⏪ **Undo** — after cancelling, a toast says "Anular" — click within 6 seconds to revert.
- 📥 **CSV** — almost every list can be exported. Look for the **"⬇ Exportar CSV"** button.

## Reading the dashboard metrics

- **Marcações hoje** — total appointments today (not counting cancelled)
- **Pendentes** — appointments waiting for your confirmation. **Try to keep this at 0** by confirming quickly.
- **Confirmadas** — appointments you've already approved.
- **Faturação hoje** — money earned today (only paid bookings count).
- **Total clientes** — your customer base size.

📊 **At a glance:** if **Pendentes** is high, you're not confirming fast enough. If **Faturação** is low but appointments are high, you have unpaid completed services — chase your team to register payments.

## Tips and warnings

- ⚠️ **Don't share your admin password.** Anyone with it can change anything.
- ⚠️ **Change the team password regularly**, especially if someone leaves.
- 💡 **Before each day starts**, open the dashboard. Confirm any pending bookings.
- 💡 **End of week**: export bookings to CSV for your accountant.
- 💡 **Test a customer booking** every now and then to make sure everything still works.
- 🆘 **If something feels broken**: log out and back in. If still broken, check Firebase Console → Firestore to see if data is being saved.
